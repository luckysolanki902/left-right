A MOMENT FOR YOURSELF

Out of your head.
Onto the page.

This is a simple two-column reflection exercise. There’s no right way to fill the page, and no need to finish it.

01

Give the thought somewhere to go.
On the left, write down whatever is troubling you. A sentence, a list, a messy thought — all welcome.

02

Find a little perspective.
On the right, respond with what you can control, one small action, or something kind and realistic to remind yourself. It’s okay if there’s nothing to solve right now.
